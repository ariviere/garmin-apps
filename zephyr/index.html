Why Zephyr

Pay once, that is it

Most wind apps charge every year. Zephyr is bought once in the Connect IQ Store and never asks again. Three seasons of WindField PRO come to $36, and of Ultimate to $90. Zephyr is $9 at most, once.

Readable at 40 km/h

One big arrow and one big number, nothing else competing for the space. Up is headwind, down is tailwind, and the gust sits right there next to the speed so you know what is about to hit you on the exposed stretch.

Nothing to set up

No account, no link code, no companion app. Zephyr asks for three permissions: internet, position, and writing to the activity. If the phone drops out, it falls back to the weather your Garmin already has and marks it, so the arrow never just goes blank.

The wind stays in your ride

After the ride, Garmin Connect shows how much of it was headwind, tailwind or crosswind, the average wind and the strongest gust. So the day that felt hard has a number next to it.
